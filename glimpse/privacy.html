Privacy Policy

Glimpse: Blood Pressure Log · Last updated: 2026-11-20

Glimpse is made by Nils-Henrik Wolf (nils.h.wolf@gmail.com). This policy explains what happens with your data when you use the Glimpse app for iPhone. Glimpse is a blood pressure log: it reads the numbers on your own home blood pressure monitor from a photo, or lets you type them in. It does not measure blood pressure and is not a medical device.

The short version

  • We collect no data. Nothing you enter or photograph is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Glimpse itself makes no network connections. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDK. Reading photos, logging, charts, reports, export and Apple Health all work in airplane mode. Only buying or restoring Glimpse Pro goes through Apple's App Store, and links you tap (such as this policy) open in your browser.
  • Photos never leave your iPhone and are never stored. They are read in memory and then discarded.
  • Your readings stay on your device and, if you allow it, in Apple Health.

Photos and camera

When you take a photo of your monitor, or pick photos from your library, Glimpse reads the numbers on the display on your iPhone using Apple's on-device image frameworks (Vision and Core Image). The photo is kept in memory only while it is being read and while you check the result. Glimpse does not save the photo, does not copy it into the app and does not upload it anywhere.

Glimpse uses the system photo picker. It never asks for access to your whole photo library and only sees the photos you choose. For imported photos, Glimpse reads the date and time the photo was taken so the reading gets the right date. It keeps no other photo information.

Your readings

Your readings (systolic, diastolic, pulse, date and time, how each reading was entered and how sure the number recognition was, and the names of any people you add) are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. Glimpse also keeps its settings on the device, for example language, theme, voice, reminder times, the Apple Health switch, how many free photo readings you have used and whether Pro is active. There is no iCloud sync. A home screen widget, if you add one, reads the same on-device storage. We never receive any of it. Readings are health information; because they never leave your device, we do not process them.

You can export your readings as a CSV file or a PDF report. These files are created on your iPhone and are only shared when you choose to share them, for example with your doctor.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Glimpse saves your own readings (blood pressure and heart rate) to Apple Health. Glimpse only writes to Apple Health; it does not read your Health data. Readings for other people you add in Glimpse are never written to Apple Health. When you delete a reading in Glimpse, Glimpse also deletes the copy it saved in Apple Health, as long as Glimpse still has permission to write to Health. If you have turned that permission off, delete the reading in the Health app as well. Glimpse never uses Health data for advertising or any purpose other than saving the readings you log.

You can turn off access at any time in the Health app → your profile → Apps → Glimpse, or in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Glimpse.

Voice read-back and reminders

Voice read-back uses the speech voices built into iOS and runs on your device; the app does not use the microphone. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone.

Purchases

Subscriptions (Glimpse Pro) are sold and billed by Apple. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. The app reads the verified purchase status from StoreKit on your device to know whether your subscription is active.

Children

Glimpse is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete a reading in the app to remove it; while Glimpse has Health access, this also removes its copy in Apple Health. Delete the app, and all data stored by it is deleted from your iPhone. Readings already saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.
