Photo tips

  • Hold the iPhone straight in front of the display and let it fill most of the frame.
  • Avoid reflections from lamps and windows; tilt the monitor slightly if the display glares.
  • Numbers shown in amber are ones Glimpse is unsure about. Tap a number to correct it before saving.
  • You can always type a reading in by hand.
  • Importing older photos: Glimpse uses the date each photo was taken. Rows marked Check need a quick look before saving.

Morning and evening

Readings taken before 15:00 count as morning, later ones as evening. You can change the date and time on the confirm screen before saving.

Apple Health

Glimpse can save your own readings to Apple Health (it never reads your Health data). Turn this on or off in Glimpse → Settings, or in the Health app → your profile → Apps → Glimpse.

Subscriptions

  • Glimpse is free for 5 photo readings and unlimited readings typed in by hand. Glimpse Pro is available weekly or yearly; new subscribers get a 7-day free trial on the yearly plan.
  • Restore a purchase: Glimpse → Settings → Restore purchases (or tap Restore on the Glimpse Pro screen).
  • Cancel anytime: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Glimpse, at least 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Your readings stay on your iPhone (and in Apple Health if you allow it). Glimpse has no servers and no account. Details in the privacy policy.

Glimpse does not measure blood pressure and is not a medical device. If you feel unwell, contact your doctor or emergency services.
