What Noteling is for

Noteling is a personal diary for storing, organising and sharing blood pressure values you have already measured with your own monitor. It does not measure, diagnose or monitor any condition, does not recommend treatment and is not a medical device.

  • The values come from your own blood pressure monitor. Noteling only copies them, either from a photo of the monitor's display or as you type them.
  • Reading numbers from a photo can go wrong (glare, a blurred photo, an unusual display). Check every value before you save it, and correct it if it differs from your monitor.
  • Noteling does not sort your readings into categories or interpret them. The reference table in the app is general information from a published guideline, not an assessment of your values.
  • Noteling is not for emergencies and does not replace advice from your doctor. If you feel unwell, contact your doctor or emergency services.
