Privacy Policy

Twenty – Cube Solver · Last updated: 2026-11-19

Twenty is made by Nils Wolf (nils.h.wolf@gmail.com). This policy explains what happens with your data when you use the Twenty app for iPhone.

The short version

Twenty collects no personal data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app works without an internet connection.

Camera

Twenty uses the camera only while you scan your cube, to read the colors of its stickers. The camera images are processed live on your iPhone and are never stored, uploaded or shared. Only the resulting sticker colors are kept in the app's memory to compute the solution.

What stays on your device

Your settings (for example sounds, voice, turn speed) and the number of free cubes you have used are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. We never receive them.

Voice coach

The spoken instructions are pre-recorded audio files bundled with the app (AI-generated voices). Nothing you say or do is recorded; the app does not use the microphone.

Purchases

Subscriptions (Twenty Pro) are sold and billed by Apple. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. The app reads the verified purchase status from StoreKit on your device to know whether your subscription is active.

Support e-mail

If you write to us, we receive what you send, like any e-mail, and use it only to answer you (legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). Our mailbox is hosted by Google (Gmail), Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, which receives and stores the mails for us. Google may process data in the USA; Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. See the Google privacy policy. We delete your mail when it is no longer needed for your request, unless a statutory retention period applies.

Crash reports

If you choose in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. You control this setting; Apple handles it under its own privacy policy, and we receive nothing that identifies you.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, a subsidiary of Microsoft. When you open a page, GitHub's servers process and log your IP address, the date and time, the page requested, the referring page and your browser's user agent. GitHub needs this to deliver the page and to keep the service secure. We have no access to these logs. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure and reliable website, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR). See the GitHub privacy statement. This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

Children

Twenty is suitable for children and collects no data from anyone, including children. Purchases are handled by Apple, including Ask to Buy for family accounts.

Deleting your data

Delete the app, and all data stored by it is deleted from your iPhone.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete and restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to it and to data portability. We hold no personal data about you except an e-mail you sent us; write to us for any of these rights. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in Austria the Datenschutzbehörde (dsb.gv.at).
