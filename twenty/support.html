Scanning tips

  • Use even light; avoid strong reflections on the stickers. Tap the flashlight button if it is dark.
  • Hold the cube so one side fills the on-screen grid. Each side is captured automatically when it is steady.
  • Show the six sides in any order and any rotation. If a color was read wrong, the Fix screen lets you tap and correct it.
  • No camera handy? Settings → Enter colors by hand.

Following the moves

  • Hold the cube with white on top and green facing you, as shown before the first move.
  • Turn the layer the arrow points at. "×2" means turn it twice.
  • Made a wrong turn? Tap Rescan at the top – Twenty reads the cube again and continues from where it really is.

Sound

The voice coach and sounds can be switched on or off in Settings, where you can also choose a female or male voice. Turn up the media volume with the volume buttons while the app is open.

Subscriptions

  • Twenty Pro is available weekly or yearly (yearly with a 7-day free trial). Your first 2 scanned cubes are free.
  • Restore a purchase: Settings → Restore purchases.
  • Cancel anytime: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Twenty, at least 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
