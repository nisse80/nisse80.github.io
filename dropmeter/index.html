The ballistics app for hunters that trusts your range more than the formula.

Your clicks, from your own groups

Shoot groups at a few distances, tap the hits, and dropmeter fits one trajectory through what you measured.

Every row says what it is

Each distance on the range card is marked verified or extrapolated, with a 95 % band from your own scatter.

Plan the shot, print the card

Distance, angle, wind, temperature and pressure give you “14 clicks up” or “hold 35 cm high”. Print the card for the scope cap or the gun case.
