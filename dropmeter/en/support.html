dropmeter Support

Questions, a bug, or a range card that does not match your rifle? Write to nils.h.wolf@gmail.com. We read every message and answer as soon as we can.

Subscription

How do I start the free trial?

Open dropmeter and choose the yearly plan. New subscribers get the first 7 days free. Apple shows the price in your currency before you confirm, and charges it when the trial ends unless you cancel at least 24 hours before.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone, open Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > dropmeter, or go straight to your subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. You keep dropmeter until the end of the period you paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Apple sells and bills the subscription, so refunds go through Apple: sign in at reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the dropmeter purchase. We never see your payment details and cannot refund a purchase ourselves.

I subscribed, but the app still shows the paywall.

Tap RESTORE on the paywall. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with. If you subscribed a moment ago, give the App Store a minute and try again.

Your data

What happens to my data when the subscription ends?

Nothing is deleted. Your rifles, sessions and groups stay on your iPhone. Without a subscription the app shows the paywall, but RESTORE and EXPORT MY DATA keep working: an encrypted backup, CSV and JSON. Subscribe again and everything is where you left it.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

On the old iPhone, open Settings > Backup > Back up and keep the file in Files, iCloud Drive or a mail to yourself. Or turn on “Automatic backup to iCloud Drive” there.

On the new iPhone, install dropmeter and tap RESTORE on the paywall. Then open Settings > Backup > “Restore from file” or “Restore from iCloud Drive” and enter your passphrase.

I forgot my backup passphrase.

The passphrase cannot be recovered, not by us and not by Apple. Without it nobody can open that backup, which is the point of the encryption. If you set a hint, it is shown when you open the file. If your data is still on your iPhone, make a new backup with a new passphrase and keep that one safe.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server. The privacy policy has the details.

Problems

Which iPhones does dropmeter run on?

Any iPhone with iOS 16 or later.

How do I report a bug?

In the app, open Settings > Report a problem. It opens a mail with the app and model version filled in. Tell us what you did and what you expected; a screenshot helps. Your data is not attached; add an export only if you want to.

My range card does not match my rifle.

A range card is valid only for that rifle, that load and those conditions. Rows marked extrapolated lie beyond the distances you shot; shoot a group there to verify them. Check every card at the range before you rely on it. If a verified row is still off, write to us with an export of the session.
