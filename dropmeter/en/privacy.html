This policy explains what happens with your data when you use the dropmeter app for iPhone and when you visit this website.

The short version

The dropmeter app collects no personal data. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. We receive personal data only when you write to us, and the host of this website keeps a short server log of each visit.

What stays on your device

Your rifles, scopes, loads, sessions, impacts, settings and computed range cards are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. We never receive them, so we do not process them.

Sensors

  • Motion and barometer: if you use FROM PHONE in the planner, the app reads the phone's tilt sensor to measure the shot angle; the barometer fills in the air pressure. The readings are used on the device for that calculation and are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Face ID: only if you turn on “Remember the passphrase with Face ID” for the encrypted backup. Face ID is handled by iOS; the app never sees your biometric data.

Exports and backups

When you export a range card (PDF), CSV, JSON or an encrypted backup, the file is created on your iPhone and handed to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next (Files, Mail, AirDrop, a printer) is your choice; the service you pick handles it under its own terms. If you turn on automatic backup to iCloud Drive, the encrypted backup is stored in your own iCloud account, which Apple runs under its privacy policy. We cannot read it.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple, which acts as its own controller for the App Store and payments (Apple privacy policy). We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app reads the verified purchase status from StoreKit on your device to know whether your subscription is active. Legal basis: performance of the contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Support e-mail

If you write to us (for example with “Report a problem” in Settings), we receive your e-mail address, your message and anything you attach. A mail started from the app also contains the app version, the calculation model version and the iOS version. We use it only to answer you. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your message concerns your subscription or the use of the app, otherwise our legitimate interest in answering your question, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Our mailbox is hosted by Google (Gmail), Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, which receives and stores the mails for us. Google may process data in the USA; Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. See the Google privacy policy. We delete your mail when it is no longer needed for your request, unless a statutory retention period applies.

Crash reports

If you choose in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Legal basis is the consent you give in iOS Settings (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw there at any time. Apple handles it under its own privacy policy, and we receive nothing that identifies you.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, a subsidiary of Microsoft. When you open a page, GitHub's servers process and log your IP address, the date and time, the page requested, the referring page and your browser's user agent. GitHub needs this to deliver the page and to keep the service secure. We have no access to these logs. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure and reliable website, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

GitHub processes the data in the USA. GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, so the transfer rests on the European Commission's adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). See the GitHub privacy statement.

The website sets no cookies, has no analytics and loads nothing from other servers; the fonts are served from this site.

Deleting your data

Delete the app, and all data stored by it is deleted from your iPhone. Backups you exported or stored in your iCloud Drive remain under your control until you delete them.

Children

dropmeter is not directed at children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). We hold no personal data about you except an e-mail you sent us; write to us for any of these rights.

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): where we process your data on the basis of our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. We then stop the processing unless we can show compelling legitimate grounds or need it for legal claims.

Where processing rests on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time; this does not affect processing before the withdrawal.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. In Austria this is the Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb.gv.at.
