dropmeter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-07

dropmeter is made by Nils Wolf (nils.h.wolf@gmail.com). This policy explains what happens with your data when you use the dropmeter app for iPhone.

The short version

dropmeter collects no personal data. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

Your rifles, scopes, loads, sessions, impacts, settings and computed range cards are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. We never receive them.

Sensors

  • Motion and barometer: if you use FROM PHONE in the planner, the app reads the phone's tilt sensor to measure the shot angle; the barometer fills in the air pressure. The readings are used on the device for that calculation and are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Face ID: only if you turn on "Remember the passphrase" for the encrypted backup. Face ID is handled by iOS; the app never sees your biometric data.

Exports and backups

When you export a range card (PDF), CSV, JSON or an encrypted backup, the file is created on your iPhone and handed to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next (Files, Mail, AirDrop, a printer) is your choice. If you turn on automatic backup to iCloud Drive, the encrypted backup is stored in your own iCloud account; we cannot read it.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. The app reads the verified purchase status from StoreKit on your device to know whether your subscription is active.

Support e-mail

If you write to us (for example with "Report a problem" in Settings), we receive what you send, like any e-mail, and use it only to answer you. We delete it when it is no longer needed for your request.

Deleting your data

Delete the app, and all data stored by it is deleted from your iPhone. Backups you exported or stored in your iCloud Drive remain under your control until you delete them.

Children

dropmeter is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new date.
